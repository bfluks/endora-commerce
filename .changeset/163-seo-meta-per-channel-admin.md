The SEO screen's meta-tag editor addresses a product override by sales channel as well as language: the channel list holds the product's own sales channels, a channel offers its own languages or the whole channel, the effective values say where each one comes from (channel and language, whole channel, language, or the product itself), and **Use inherited** removes the override at exactly the selected scope. The admin meta read answers `sources` beside `resolved`. Categories keep the language rows only.
