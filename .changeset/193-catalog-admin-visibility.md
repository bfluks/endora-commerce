`@endora-commerce/mod-catalog` narrows its admin product reads by `product_visibility_rules`' admin scope while that module is on (spec 193): the product list (page, `total` and the tab badges, cut in the statement that pages), `resolve-ids`, `batch-by-id`, a product's variants and the admin global search (`hasMore` over visible rows) answer only the products and variants the caller's role may see, and the list answers `visibility: { restricted: true, reason?: 'no_category', categoryName? }` to a restricted caller (absent otherwise). Every route addressed by a product id answers a hidden product — and a hidden variant of a visible product — exactly as one that does not exist, writes nothing, and no API pre-interceptor sees its id. The list, selection, batch and variant answers are sent `Cache-Control: private, no-store` whatever the module's state. With the module off or absent nothing is narrowed. `CatalogAdminService` list reads and `CatalogAdminSearchService.searchProducts` take the scope as a required first parameter; `adminProductVisibilityPort` is declared `degrades-without`.

`@endora-commerce/contracts` adds `adminProductListVisibilitySchema`, `AdminProductListVisibility` and `adminProductListVisibility(scope)`.

Also narrowed for a restricted caller (spec 193 Phase 5b): bulk updates (a hidden id goes the way a missing one does, before it is applied or queued; a queued run re-checks each product for its requester at its write), the bulk-operation list, detail and undo (an operation touching a hidden product is, to them, an unknown one), a product's links, grouped items and bundle options (a hidden target is left out, and naming one answers target-not-found), an attribute set's `productCount` and its in-use refusal, an attribute's usage and scope-change preview, and the assistant tools (scope resolved for the invoking admin at preview and again at confirm). `URL_KEY_TAKEN` and `URL_KEY_REDIRECT_TAKEOVER_REQUIRED` name a holder hidden from the caller by key and channel only: `@endora-commerce/contracts`' `heldByProductId` / `heldByProductName` and `losingProductId` / `losingProductName` become optional. The published `productLinkService`, `groupedService`, `bundleService` and `attributeSetService` ports answer as the system, unchanged.

**Type change for consumers of `@endora-commerce/contracts`:** `UrlKeyTakenDetails.conflicts[].heldByProductId` / `heldByProductName` and `UrlKeyRedirectTakeoverRequiredDetails.takeovers[].losingProductId` / `losingProductName` were `string` and are now `string | undefined` (optional). Code that reads them must handle their absence — absent means the holder is hidden from the caller, and the answer names the key and channel only (the catalog admin's own readers, `product-url-keys-api.ts` and `ProductEditor.tsx`, already do). `minor`, as the package is in `0.x` (D-225); HTTP error shapes are versioned under Principle II, not by `specs/conventions/contract-surface.md`.

A URL key held by a hidden product's **redirect** is, to a restricted operator, simply taken (owner ruling 2026-11-26): `PUT …/products/:id/url-keys` (and the scoped-values save) answers `409 URL_KEY_TAKEN` by key and channel, never `URL_KEY_REDIRECT_TAKEOVER_REQUIRED`, and a `confirmTakeover` naming the key is refused the same way; the hidden product's redirect is kept. Unrestricted operators keep the takeover.
