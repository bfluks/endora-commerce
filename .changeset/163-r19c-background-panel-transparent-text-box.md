`cms.BackgroundPanel` gains **Transparent text box** (`textBoxTransparent`, a switch, off by default). On, the panel carries `data-text-box="transparent"` and its text box paints no background of its own, so the image shows through behind the text. Off or unset, the panel renders as before. English and Polish labels in `@endora-commerce/mod-cms`.
