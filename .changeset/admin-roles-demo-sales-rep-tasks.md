The demo's `sales_representative` role also holds `tasks:read` and `tasks:write`. A demo Sales Rep
can now open *My Tasks* and work the Tasks assigned to them, and is offered when a colleague
assigns a Task or mentions a person with `@`. Only the demo data changes: roles on an instance
that sells for real are untouched.
