A product's storefront page follows its availability at once. `catalog` now drops the storefront's cached product-page reads of a product — tagged by its id, slug, URL keys and former keys — after `product.updated.v1`, `product.archived.v1` and a product's `sales_channels.membership_changed`, and its keys after `product.deleted.v1`, through the storefront revalidate endpoint it already uses for categories (`STOREFRONT_BASE_URL` + `REVALIDATE_SECRET`; a no-op without them). Any `product.updated.v1` counts — a gallery, attachment, packaging-unit or name change drops the product's page as well, since the event does not say which writes change availability. A short trailing debounce (150 ms, at most 1 s) gathers a bulk operation into one call, chunked at 200 tags; past 500 pending products the shared `catalog:product` tag is dropped once instead; one call is in flight at a time. `product.deleted.v1` now carries `formerUrlKeys`, the redirect keys the deletion removed, so a deleted product's pages are dropped by key too (the shared tag only when its keys cannot be read). Best-effort: a failed key read or call is logged and never fails the write. Next keeps only a `200` in its Data Cache, so a product switched off, archived or moved out of a channel used to go on showing until the storefront restarted. The tag vocabulary is `CATALOG_PRODUCT_STOREFRONT_CACHE_TAGS` (`product`, `productKey(key)`, `maxLength`), shared by both sides.
