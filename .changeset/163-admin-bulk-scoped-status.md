The products bulk edit offers **Set status in one channel**: a sales channel, optionally one of its languages, and Active, Inactive or Use inherited. It cannot be combined with Status — each is disabled while the other is chosen, and says why. The result names the scope it set, lists the products skipped because they are not in the channel (never added to it) or refused by the completeness check, and those switched on with values missing; the result table's headings and statuses are translated.
