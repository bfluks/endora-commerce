The website rule (spec 193, Phase 6). `@endora-commerce/mod-product-visibility-rules` saves named product rules (`POST|PATCH|DELETE /api/v1/admin/product-visibility/product-rules`; boolean and choice attributes that are neither channel- nor language-scoped, listed by `GET …/attributes`, and catalogue-price conditions) and lets one rule govern a sales channel's product membership. Attaching a rule, or changing an attached rule's definition, goes through a preview (`POST …/website-rules/previews`, polled with `GET …/previews/:id`): a job evaluates it over every product and stores the join / leave snapshot, only its creator can confirm it (`POST …/previews/:id/confirm`) within 15 minutes, and the apply job writes exactly that snapshot through the kernel's membership governor, attributed `website_rule:<ruleId>`. Inactive, archived and deleted products never join. `DELETE …/website-rules/attachments/:salesChannelId` detaches a rule and keeps the membership. The steady state re-evaluates products marked by catalogue events every 30 s, reconciles every governed channel hourly and at the window boundaries of a designated book. New error codes in `@endora-commerce/contracts`: `PRODUCT_RULE_ATTACHED`, `WEBSITE_RULE_PREVIEW_REQUIRED`, `WEBSITE_RULE_PREVIEW_LIMIT`, `WEBSITE_RULE_PREVIEW_STALE`, `CHANNEL_ALREADY_GOVERNED`, and the schemas `productRuleSchema`, `productRuleAttributesResponseSchema`, `websiteRulePreviewCreatedSchema` with `websiteRuleAuditSource`.
